# Observations

1. Most passengers did not survive the disaster.
2. Male passengers were more than female passengers.
3. Most passengers were between 20 and 40 years old.
4. First-class passengers had a higher survival rate than third-class passengers.
5. Fare prices varied significantly among passengers.

# Conclusion

This exploratory data analysis helped identify patterns in passenger demographics and survival. Passenger class, gender, and fare appeared to influence survival outcomes.
